# Maryland brand: provisional (2026-11-03), waiting for Nick's calls

Built while Nick was away, so every choice below is a placeholder in the pattern he picked for the other states. Changing any of
them is cheap: the display name and copy, `scripts/make-brand.swift` (icon, favicons, share image), `Design/Theme.swift`, the site
palette in `scripts/make-site.py`, then the screenshots again.

## Name (Nick decides)
- **Used for now: "Maryland Eats: Restaurants"**, Home Screen **"MD Eats"**, subtitle **"Crab Cakes, Pit Beef & Oysters"** (30/30).
  The prompt's lead (no App Store match in September 2026; not rechecked, no trademark search run).
- **Alternatives from the prompt:** "Old Line Eats" (many businesses use the nickname: check marks), "Chesapeake Eats" (the Bay is
  Virginia's too). Avoid: "Old Bay" (McCormick), "True Blue" (a state mark), "Charm City" (Baltimore only), "Natty Boh" (Pabst),
  "Maryland Fried Chicken" (a chain) and the tourism slogans.
- Bundle id `com.marylandeats.ios` and repo name `maryland-eats` are neutral; they don't need to change with the name.

## Palette (provisional): the flag
Calvert black #1A1A1A (17:1 on white) for titles and accents; Crossland red #C8102E as the guide-card fill under white text (5.9:1);
Calvert gold #F2B705 only as a fill under dark text (gold text on white is 1.8:1); warm-white #FAF6EC page. The header band is the
flag's Calvert quarters: gold and black pales, counterchanged. No team colors (Ravens and Orioles vs. Washington's teams split the
state). The prompt's alternative: a Chesapeake set (bay blue #1F4E6B, crab orange #D9532B, used now only in the icon's crab).
