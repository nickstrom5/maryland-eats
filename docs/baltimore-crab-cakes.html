Baltimore crab cakes & crab houses

11 places for Maryland's crab cakes, jumbo lump and backfin with little filler, broiled or fried, and for crabs steamed and seasoned by the dozen. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Ask for early accessThe free Maryland Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Broiled, fried or steamed

A Maryland crab cake is mostly crab: lump or backfin meat held together with as little as the kitchen can manage. Faidley's has sold them at Lexington Market since 1886, and Jimmy's on Holabird Avenue since 1974. Steamed crabs are a warm-weather meal at the crab houses on this list; the crab cakes are there year-round.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Baltimore (8), Dundalk (1), Linthicum Heights (1), Parkville (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Crab houses and snowball stands keep seasons, and Ocean City closes for the winter, so check before you drive.
