What counts as an honor here

One source, reported as fact rather than score: the James Beard Foundation's own announcements, winners and semifinalists alike. Charleston in Harbor East won Outstanding Wine and Other Beverages Program in 2025. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.
