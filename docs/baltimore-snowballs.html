Baltimore snowballs

16 snowball stands: finely shaved ice soaked in flavored syrup, Baltimore's summer treat, from Hampden to Bel Air. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Ask for early accessThe free Maryland Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

A summer thing

Every stand here is in Baltimore Magazine's May 2026 snowball guide. Some date back decades: The Snowball Stand in Woodstock to 1974, Snoasis to 1977, Emmorton in Bel Air to 1981. Most open in spring and close in the fall, so a dark stand in winter is seasonal, not closed.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Baltimore (5), Lutherville-Timonium (2), Reisterstown (1), Bel Air (1), Parkville (1), Severna Park (1), Catonsville (1), Dundalk (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Crab houses and snowball stands keep seasons, and Ocean City closes for the winter, so check before you drive.
