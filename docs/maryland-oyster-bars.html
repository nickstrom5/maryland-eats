Maryland oyster bars

8 places for Chesapeake oysters: raw at the bar, roasted, fried, or in stew, from Baltimore's waterfront to Annapolis and the Eastern Shore. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Ask for early accessThe free Maryland Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Raw, roasted, fried

The Baltimore places here won Baltimore Magazine's 2026 oyster categories, from raw oysters at Thames Street to roasted ones at True Chesapeake, which farms its own. The Eastern Shore places are on Talbot County's oyster trail through Easton, St. Michaels and Tilghman Island.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Baltimore (3), St. Michaels (2), Easton (1), Annapolis (1), Tilghman (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Crab houses and snowball stands keep seasons, and Ocean City closes for the winter, so check before you drive.

On your phone, Maryland Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
